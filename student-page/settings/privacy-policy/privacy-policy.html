Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

The Student Internship Records and Performance Monitoring System respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting the personal information of students, coordinators, supervisors, and other authorized users.

This Privacy Policy explains how the system collects, uses, stores, protects, and manages personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and applicable regulations of the National Privacy Commission.

Information We Collect

The system may collect and process the following information:

Student Information

  • Full name
  • Student ID
  • Email address
  • Section or program
  • Contact information, when applicable
  • Internship status
  • Assigned company and supervisor

Attendance Information

  • Date and time of Time In and Time Out
  • Attendance status
  • Rendered internship hours
  • Attendance history
  • Attendance remarks
  • Photo proof submitted during attendance

Internship Information

  • Daily tasks or activities
  • Internship progress
  • Performance evaluation records
  • Skills and competency information
  • Internship completion status
  • Other information necessary for internship monitoring and reporting

Photo Proof Collection

The system collects a photo proof during the student's attendance process to help verify that the registered student is physically present when recording Time In or Time Out.

The photo proof may be associated with the student's attendance record, date, time, and other information necessary for attendance verification.

The photo will be processed only for legitimate internship-related purposes such as:

  • Verifying attendance;
  • Preventing false or fraudulent attendance records;
  • Maintaining accurate internship records;
  • Monitoring student internship participation; and
  • Supporting attendance reports and related evaluations.

The photo proof will not be used for unrelated purposes or publicly displayed without an appropriate lawful basis or consent.

Purpose of Data Processing

The personal information collected by the system may be used to:

  • Create and manage student internship accounts;
  • Record and verify attendance;
  • Calculate rendered internship hours;
  • Monitor daily internship activities and progress;
  • Record performance evaluations;
  • Identify students who may require intervention or assistance;
  • Generate internship-related reports;
  • Support the internship coordinator and authorized personnel in managing the practicum program; and
  • Maintain accurate records required for internship administration.

Legal Basis for Processing

Personal information will be processed only when there is a lawful basis under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, such as consent, compliance with a legal or institutional obligation, fulfillment of an agreement, or another applicable lawful basis.

Where consent is required, students will be informed of the purpose and extent of the processing before their information is collected.

Access to Personal Information

Access to student information is limited to authorized individuals who need the information for legitimate internship-related purposes.

Depending on their role, authorized users may include:

  • Internship coordinators;
  • Authorized school personnel;
  • Assigned company supervisors; and
  • Other authorized personnel involved in the administration and evaluation of the internship program.

Personal information will not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals or organizations unless disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Data Storage and Security

The system implements reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction.

Access to the system and its records is controlled according to user roles and permissions.

Reasonable security measures will also be applied to the storage and management of attendance photos and other student records.

Data Retention

Personal information, attendance records, photo proofs, and internship records will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which they were collected, comply with applicable school policies, or satisfy legal and legitimate record-keeping requirements.

When the information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted, destroyed, or anonymized when appropriate.

Student Rights

As a data subject, students have rights under the Data Privacy Act, including the right to:

  • Be informed about the processing of their personal information;
  • Access their personal information;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Object to certain processing, when applicable;
  • Withdraw consent when consent is the lawful basis for processing, subject to applicable limitations;
  • Request the deletion or blocking of personal information when legally applicable; and
  • File a complaint regarding the processing of their personal information.

These rights are subject to the limitations and conditions provided by applicable laws and regulations.

Data Breach and Security Incidents

In the event of a data breach or security incident involving personal information, appropriate measures will be taken to investigate, contain, and address the incident in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and institutional procedures.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes in the system, internship processes, applicable laws, or data processing practices.

Students will be informed of significant changes when appropriate.

By using the system, students acknowledge that they have been informed about how their personal information is collected and processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
