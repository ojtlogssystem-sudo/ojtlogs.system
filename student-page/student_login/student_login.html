Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy

Notice: Before proceeding, please read the following Terms and Conditions and Data Privacy Statement carefully. By clicking "I Agree," you consent to the collection, processing, and storage of your personal data for official academic and internship monitoring purposes.

Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using the OJT-LOGS platform, you agree to comply with and be bound by the guidelines established by Global Reciprocal Colleges (GRC) regarding Student Internship / On-the-Job Training (OJT) monitoring.

Collection of Personal & Academic Information

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), OJT-LOGS collects and processes limited personal identifiable information (PII) necessary for tracking your internship progress, including:

  • Full Name, Student ID Number, Program/Course, and Institutional Email Address.
  • Company details and Supervisor contact information.
  • Daily Task Summaries, Rendered Hours, and Attendance Logs.

Photo Capture & Attendance Verification

To ensure log integrity and prevent fraudulent attendance practices (e.g., proxy clock-ins):

  • The system requires temporary access to your device camera to capture a live verification photo during QR Code scanning upon clock-in.
  • Captured images are used strictly for verification and auditing purposes by the OJT Coordinator and System Administrator and will not be shared with unauthorized third parties.

Analytics and Skill Matching

  • Performance metrics, total rendered hours, and task categories will be analyzed by the platform's Predictive Analytics module to flag potential completion delays early.
  • De-identified and aggregated historical task data may be used by the Skill Gap Analysis module to assist future student batches in identifying compatible internship placements.

Data Security and Retention

  • All student records, attendance logs, and evaluations are securely stored in the OJT-LOGS database.
  • Upon completion of your OJT requirements and academic clearance, student profiles will be archived by batch in accordance with GRC institutional data retention policies and RA 10173.

User Responsibility

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.
  • Any deliberate attempt to submit falsified attendance logs, manipulate QR codes, or alter verification images will be subject to academic disciplinary action under the GRC Student Handbook.
