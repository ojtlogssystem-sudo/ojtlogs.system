OJT-LOGS: Coordinator Data Protection & Administrative Agreement

Notice: As an authorized OJT Coordinator and System Administrator, your access to the OJT-LOGS platform grants you authority to handle confidential student data. By accessing this system, you agree to adhere to the provisions of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and GRC administrative standards.

1. Scope of Authorized Access

  • Access is granted solely for monitoring student attendance, validating rendered hours, reviewing supervisor evaluations, managing partner company profiles, and conducting academic performance audits.
  • Credentials and administrative access must not be shared, transferred, or delegated to unauthorized individuals.

2. Confidentiality & Data Protection (RA 10173 Compliance)

  • All student profile records, daily attendance logs, verification photographs, and performance analytics are strictly confidential.
  • Administrators must refrain from exporting, copying, or distributing student data outside official academic channels or downloading records onto unsecured personal devices.

3. Analytics & Decision Support Responsibility

  • Data generated by the Predictive Analytics (At-Risk Interns) and Diagnostic Analytics (Skill Gap Analysis) modules serve strictly as decision-support tools.
  • Administrative interventions (e.g., counseling, student follow-ups, or company recommendations) must remain unbiased, academically justified, and guided by institutional policies.

4. Record Auditability and Archiving

  • All administrative actions, including manual adjustments to student logs, evaluation overrides, and batch archiving, are tracked in the system's audit trail.
  • Official exported reports (e.g., PDF summary logs and clearance sheets) must be stored and handled in compliance with GRC data retention policies.

5. System Integrity & Misuse

  • Deliberate alteration of verified student records, falsification of completion status, or misuse of administrative privileges will constitute a breach of institutional policy and data protection regulations.
